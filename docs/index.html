Privacy Policy

Rhotacism Therapy  ·  Last updated: September 9, 2026

Rhotacism Therapy is a speech therapy practice app that helps users improve their pronunciation of the /r/ sound. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

The app records short audio clips of your voice when you practice a word. These recordings are:

  • Sent to our analysis server to evaluate your pronunciation
  • Processed in real time and not stored after analysis is complete
  • Never shared with third parties

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal identifying information. No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

How We Use Your Audio

Audio recordings are used solely to:

  • Transcribe the spoken word using a speech recognition model
  • Analyze the acoustic properties of your /r/ pronunciation
  • Return immediate feedback to you within the app

Recordings are discarded immediately after analysis. We do not build profiles, train models on your voice, or retain audio data.

Data Transmission

Audio is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our analysis server hosted on Railway. The server does not log or persist audio files.

Children's Privacy

Rhotacism Therapy is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect personal information of any kind, no special handling is required for users under 13.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
